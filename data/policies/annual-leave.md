# Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains annual-leave eligibility, accrual, requests, carryover, and balance calculations. It applies to permanent and fixed-term employees. Intern arrangements are defined in their placement terms.

The policy year runs from 1 January through 31 December. Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave when they fall on a day the employee would otherwise have worked.

## 2. Annual entitlement

A full-time employee receives 20 working days for a complete policy year. A part-time employee's full-year entitlement equals 20 days multiplied by the employee's contracted work-schedule percentage. The result is rounded to the nearest half-day only after the calculation is complete.

Job level does not change the base entitlement. A manager cannot grant a different recurring allowance without a written People Operations exception.

## 3. First-year proration

An employee who starts during the year receives a prorated first-year entitlement:

```text
full_year_entitlement = 20 × work_schedule_percentage
first_year_entitlement = full_year_entitlement × employed_calendar_days / days_in_policy_year
```

`employed_calendar_days` includes the employee's start date through 31 December. Use 365 or 366 days according to the relevant policy year. Do not round intermediate values; round the final entitlement to the nearest 0.5 day.

Example: a full-time employee who starts on 1 July in a 365-day year is employed for 184 calendar days. The unrounded entitlement is `20 × 184 / 365`, which is then rounded to the nearest half-day.

## 4. Accrual and balance definitions

Leave accrues monthly and is credited on the final day of each month. Three values must be distinguished:

- **Accrued balance**: leave credited so far, plus valid carryover, less approved leave.
- **Projected year-end entitlement**: the estimated total entitlement if employment and work schedule remain unchanged.
- **Available balance**: accrued leave plus valid carryover, less approved leave already taken and approved future leave.

```text
available_balance = accrued_leave + valid_carryover
                    - approved_leave_taken - approved_future_leave
```

Every personalized balance answer must identify the employee record used and state an `as_of_date`. A pending request is not deducted until it is approved.

## 5. Taking leave during probation

An employee in probation may use up to three days from their accrued balance. A request exceeding three days during probation requires People Operations approval in addition to manager approval.

This rule does not provide an advance balance. If the employee has accrued fewer than three days, the lower accrued amount remains the normal maximum unless People Operations approves an exception.

## 6. Request and approval process

Annual leave requires manager approval. Employees should normally provide at least five working days' notice. A request for five or more consecutive working days should normally be made at least 20 calendar days in advance.

These notice periods are planning expectations, not automatic rejection rules. Managers consider coverage, conflicting requests, and operational needs. Approval must not be assumed until it is recorded.

The minimum request unit is half a working day. Requests for a smaller unit should be corrected rather than rounded automatically.

## 7. Carryover

Up to five unused days may carry into the next policy year. Carried days must be used by 31 March and expire on 1 April. Current-year leave is not used ahead of valid carryover unless the leave system explicitly records a different order.

A manager cannot restore expired carryover. Any proposed restoration requires People Operations review and a recorded exception.

## 8. Negative balances and changes

Negative annual-leave balances are not normally permitted. People Operations may approve up to three negative days in exceptional circumstances. A manager alone cannot approve a negative balance.

Changes to work schedule may alter projected entitlement. The HR system must recalculate the entitlement using effective-dated schedule data; the assistant must not assume that the latest percentage applied for the whole year.

## 9. Leaving Northstar

People Operations calculates final entitlement and any treatment of unused or excess leave according to the employee record and applicable location requirements. The HR assistant may explain the method but must not provide a final payout or deduction amount.

## 10. Exceptions and escalation

Escalate to People Operations when:

- A user disputes the official balance or dates.
- A request would create a negative balance.
- More than three days are requested during probation.
- A work schedule changed during the year.
- Carryover has expired or a restoration is requested.
- The employee's location or employment status is unclear.
- The global policy and location addendum appear inconsistent.
