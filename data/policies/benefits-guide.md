# Employee Benefits Guide

## 1. Purpose and scope

This guide summarizes the synthetic Standard and Premium benefit plans offered to eligible Hong Kong employees. It is an administrative comparison, not medical advice or a guarantee that an insurer will approve a claim.

Permanent and fixed-term employees are eligible unless their employment record states otherwise. Intern and contractor arrangements are outside this guide.

## 2. Coverage start

Coverage begins on the first day of the month after the employee's start date. If the employee starts on the first day of a month, coverage begins that day.

Examples:

- An employee starting 15 June has a coverage start date of 1 July.
- An employee starting 1 June has a coverage start date of 1 June.

The employee should not use benefits before enrollment is confirmed in the benefits record.

## 3. Plan comparison

| Feature | Standard | Premium |
|---|---:|---:|
| Employee monthly contribution | HKD 0 | HKD 420 |
| Outpatient annual limit | HKD 6,000 | HKD 12,000 |
| Specialist co-pay | HKD 250 per eligible visit | HKD 100 per eligible visit |
| Dental annual limit | HKD 1,500 | HKD 4,000 |
| Vision annual limit | Not included | HKD 1,500 |
| Dependants | Employee-paid add-on | One dependant subsidized at 50% |

All limits are per policy year unless the insurer terms state otherwise. A higher annual limit does not mean every service is covered.

## 4. Enrollment and plan selection

The Standard plan is the default. An eligible employee may elect Premium within 30 calendar days of becoming eligible. The deadline is calculated from the coverage eligibility date, not necessarily the start date.

After the initial window, plan changes are allowed during the annual enrollment period or following a qualifying life event confirmed by People Operations. The assistant may identify a potential event but cannot determine eligibility or change enrollment.

## 5. Dependants

Under Standard, dependant coverage is an employee-paid add-on. Under Premium, one eligible dependant receives a 50% subsidy on the applicable dependant premium. Additional dependants are employee-paid.

Dependant eligibility and evidence are verified by People Operations and the plan administrator. The assistant must not request identity or medical documents in chat.

## 6. Contributions and payroll

Premium's HKD 420 monthly employee contribution is deducted through payroll. Partial-month treatment, missed deductions, refunds, and payroll disputes must be referred to Payroll or People Operations.

The assistant may compare published monthly contributions but must not calculate tax treatment or guarantee a payroll amount.

## 7. Claims and privacy

Claims are administered by the insurer according to plan terms. Northstar does not use diagnosis or claim details for routine HR policy answers.

The assistant must not access, request, or interpret diagnoses, recommend treatment, assess medical necessity, or predict a claim decision. A denied or disputed claim should be referred to the plan administrator through the secure channel.

## 8. Leave and employment changes

Benefits normally continue during company-paid parental leave, subject to plan terms and required employee contributions. Coverage during unpaid leave, extended absence, changes in schedule, or the end of employment requires People Operations review.

## 9. Escalation

Refer to People Operations or the plan administrator when:

- The eligibility or coverage-start record is missing or disputed.
- The plan-selection deadline may have passed.
- A qualifying life event is claimed.
- Dependant eligibility is uncertain.
- A contribution or claim is disputed.
- Medical or other protected information is involved.
