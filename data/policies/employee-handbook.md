# Employee Handbook

## 1. Purpose

This handbook introduces the working practices and employment policies of Northstar Systems Ltd. (Northstar). It is a summary, not a replacement for the detailed policies referenced below. Where this handbook differs from a policy or location addendum, the more specific and higher-priority document applies.

Northstar is a fictional enterprise software and managed-services company. This handbook and all related records are synthetic demo data.

## 2. Who this handbook covers

This handbook applies to permanent employees, fixed-term employees, interns, and other workers where a section expressly includes them. Individual eligibility may depend on work location, employment type, work schedule, start date, probation status, or benefit enrollment.

Contractors should consult their service agreement. They must follow the Code of Conduct and information-security requirements, but they do not receive employee benefits merely because those policies appear in this handbook.

## 3. Policy hierarchy

Northstar applies policy sources in this order:

1. A mandatory location addendum, including HR-LOC-HK-001 for Hong Kong.
2. A written, case-specific exception approved by People Operations.
3. The detailed global policy for the relevant topic.
4. This handbook summary.

A manager may approve requests within a policy but cannot waive eligibility rules, statutory requirements, or a required People Operations review. If two sources conflict, employees should not choose the more favorable rule themselves; they should ask People Operations to resolve the conflict.

## 4. Working arrangements

Northstar's standard working week is Monday through Friday, and a standard full-time working day is eight hours. Local public holidays and an employee's agreed schedule may vary.

Office-based hybrid employees may request up to two remote-working days per working week. During probation, the normal limit is one day per week. These are maximums rather than automatic entitlements. See HR-WORK-001.

Working temporarily from another country is governed separately by HR-WORK-002. An employee must not treat personal travel, annual leave, business travel, and international remote work as interchangeable categories.

## 5. Time away from work

Full-time employees normally receive 20 working days of annual leave per policy year, prorated for eligible part-time schedules and first-year service. Leave is accrued monthly and requires approval. Carryover and probation rules are set out in HR-LEAVE-001.

Sick and medical leave is separate from annual leave. Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and normally before their scheduled start. Medical information must be handled confidentially. See HR-LEAVE-002 and the applicable location addendum.

Eligible primary and supporting caregivers may receive company-paid parental leave under HR-LEAVE-003. All parental-leave guidance is preliminary until People Operations confirms eligibility.

## 6. Expenses and benefits

Northstar reimburses reasonable, necessary business expenses that satisfy HR-EXP-001. Being below a monetary limit does not make a personal expense reimbursable. Employees cannot approve their own claims, and splitting one purchase to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.

Eligible employees are enrolled in benefits according to HR-BEN-001 and the applicable location rules. Published plan comparisons explain administrative features only and do not constitute medical or financial advice.

## 7. Expected conduct

Everyone working with Northstar must act professionally, protect confidential information, avoid retaliation, disclose relevant conflicts of interest, and comply with HR-CONDUCT-001.

Northstar provides reporting routes for harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety risks, and suspected fraud. The company prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern or participates in a review.

## 8. Getting help

General policy questions may be directed to the employee's manager or People Operations. Sensitive concerns may be raised through a People Partner or the confidential ethics channel described in HR-SUPPORT-001.

For an immediate threat to safety, contact local emergency services first and then company Security when safe to do so.

The HR assistant may summarize policies, calculate synthetic entitlements, and identify approval routes. It cannot approve requests, change records, make employment decisions, give legal or medical advice, or investigate allegations.
