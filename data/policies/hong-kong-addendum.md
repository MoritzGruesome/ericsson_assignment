# Hong Kong Location Addendum

## 1. Purpose and precedence

This addendum identifies how Northstar's global HR policies apply to employees whose assigned work location is Hong Kong. It is synthetic demo data and is not a statement of Hong Kong law.

If this addendum conflicts with the Employee Handbook or a global policy, this addendum takes precedence. If this addendum is silent, the applicable global policy continues to apply. Questions about statutory rights or legal compliance must be referred to People Operations and must not be answered from this demo document.

## 2. Working arrangements

The standard full-time schedule is Monday through Friday at eight hours per day. The employee record contains the assigned office, work schedule, and any approved alternative arrangement.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 Hong Kong time for employees assigned to Hong Kong unless a team schedule or recorded accommodation states otherwise.

## 3. Annual leave

The Hong Kong company allowance is 20 working days for a full policy year. First-year and part-time entitlements are prorated under HR-LEAVE-001. The normal carryover maximum is five days, usable through 31 March of the following year.

During probation, an employee may use up to three days from accrued annual leave. More than three days requires People Operations approval. This does not guarantee an advance balance.

Public holidays applicable to the employee record do not consume annual leave when they fall on a scheduled working day. The assistant must use the structured holiday calendar for date calculations rather than inventing public-holiday dates.

## 4. Sick and medical leave

An employee may self-certify one consecutive scheduled working day. A medical certificate is required from the second consecutive scheduled working day.

Paid sick-leave entitlement must be read from the dedicated Hong Kong entitlement record. The assistant must not derive or summarize a statutory formula, and it must not substitute annual leave when the sick-leave record is unavailable.

An absence of five or more consecutive scheduled working days must be referred to Hong Kong People Operations for case management.

## 5. Parental leave

Northstar's company program provides 16 weeks of company-paid leave for an eligible primary caregiver and four weeks for an eligible supporting caregiver, subject to HR-LEAVE-003 and People Operations confirmation.

Any mandatory local rights are administered by Hong Kong People Operations. The assistant must not determine statutory eligibility or calculate a legal entitlement from this addendum.

## 6. Domestic remote work

Hong Kong office-based hybrid employees may request up to two domestic remote days per working week. During probation, the normal maximum is one day per week. Manager approval and the eligibility record are required.

A request linked to health, disability, caregiving, or another sensitive circumstance uses the confidential People Operations process and should not be evaluated as an ordinary preference request.

## 7. International remote work

Hong Kong-assigned permanent employees who have completed probation may request up to 20 working days abroad per policy year, with no more than 10 working days in a single trip. Requests normally require 30 calendar days' notice, manager approval, and People Operations approval.

Destination, immigration, tax, security, customer, and data-access reviews remain mandatory where applicable. This addendum does not confirm that an employee may legally work in any destination.

## 8. Expenses and currency

Hong Kong dollar thresholds in HR-EXP-001 apply unless an effective-dated destination table provides another rule. Key thresholds include:

- Taxi or ride-hailing: HKD 500 per trip.
- Domestic travel meals: HKD 350 per day.
- International travel meals: HKD 600 per day unless a lower destination cap applies.
- Hong Kong hotels: HKD 1,800 per night.
- Client entertainment: HKD 1,500 per event.
- Approved home-office accessories: HKD 1,500 per policy year.

Above-limit expenses require the approvals and evidence stated in HR-EXP-001. A local threshold does not remove the business-purpose requirement.

## 9. Benefits

Eligible Hong Kong employees may participate in the Standard or Premium plan in HR-BEN-001. Standard is the default. Premium costs the employee HKD 420 per month under the 2026 synthetic plan.

Coverage begins on the first day of the month after the employee starts, or on the start date when that date is the first day of a month. Premium must normally be elected within 30 calendar days of eligibility.

## 10. Support routes

General questions and record corrections go to `people-help@northstar.example`. Sensitive employment matters and policy exceptions go to `hk-people-partner@northstar.example`. These are fictional addresses.

An immediate safety threat should be directed to local emergency services first, followed by Hong Kong office Security when safe.
