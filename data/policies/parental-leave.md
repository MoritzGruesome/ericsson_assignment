# Parental Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides company-paid leave for eligible employees welcoming a child through birth or adoption. It uses the inclusive terms **primary caregiver** and **supporting caregiver**. Location-specific rights may supplement these company benefits.

All answers about parental leave are preliminary until People Operations verifies eligibility and dates.

## 2. Eligibility

A permanent or fixed-term employee is eligible when they will have completed 26 weeks of continuous service by the expected birth or placement date. Interns are not covered by the company-paid program unless their placement terms say otherwise.

A break in service, uncertain expected date, or contract ending during the proposed leave period must be reviewed by People Operations. The assistant must not resolve those cases by inference.

## 3. Company-paid leave

An eligible primary caregiver may receive 16 weeks of company-paid parental leave. An eligible supporting caregiver may receive four weeks of company-paid parental leave.

For one event, an employee cannot be classified as both primary and supporting caregiver to combine entitlements. Multiple births or multiple children placed in one adoption are treated as one event for the company-paid allowance.

The location addendum may provide additional rights. Where a mandatory local entitlement is more favorable, the applicable location rule takes precedence.

## 4. Notice and request process

An employee should notify People Operations at least eight weeks before the intended leave start, where practicable. The request should include the expected event date, intended start date, expected duration, and caregiver classification.

Late notice does not automatically remove eligibility. People Operations will consider circumstances and operational arrangements.

If dates change, the employee should notify People Operations promptly. A manager or the HR assistant cannot finally approve a changed schedule.

## 5. Annual leave and benefits

Annual leave continues to accrue during company-paid parental leave. Approved annual leave may be taken before or after parental leave if separately requested and approved.

Benefits normally continue during company-paid leave, subject to plan terms and required employee contributions. Employees should consult HR-BEN-001 and People Operations for coverage during any unpaid period.

## 6. Return to work

People Operations and the employee's manager should discuss the expected return date and any handover arrangements. A request for flexible work after leave is considered under HR-WORK-001 and is not automatically approved by this policy.

Requests connected to health, disability, breastfeeding facilities, or other sensitive circumstances must use the confidential People Operations process.

## 7. Privacy

Supporting documents are handled only through approved People Operations systems. The assistant must not ask the employee to upload medical evidence or adoption records in chat.

Managers receive only information needed for workforce planning. They should not request diagnostic or other unnecessary sensitive details.

## 8. Exceptions and escalation

People Operations review is mandatory when:

- Continuous service or employee status is unclear.
- A fixed-term contract may end during leave.
- Primary or supporting caregiver classification is disputed.
- Two leave types overlap.
- Dates change materially after approval.
- The employee requests an exception or a location-specific right.
- Sensitive supporting information is involved.
