# Sick and Medical Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how employees report sickness, when supporting documentation is needed, and how Northstar protects medical privacy. Paid sick-leave entitlement is location-specific and must be read with the applicable addendum and employee record.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. An employee should not be instructed to use annual leave merely because a sick-leave balance cannot be confirmed immediately.

## 2. Reporting an absence

An employee who cannot work because of illness or injury should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible and normally before their scheduled start. The notification should state that the employee is unable to work, the expected duration if known, and any urgent handover needs.

The employee is not required to give their manager a diagnosis. If the employee cannot report personally, another person may make initial contact on their behalf.

A missed or late notification may require follow-up, but it is not by itself proof of misconduct.

## 3. Documentation

An employee may self-certify one consecutive scheduled working day. A medical certificate is required from the second consecutive scheduled working day of absence.

Consecutive days are counted using the employee's scheduled working days, not calendar days. For example, a Friday and the following Monday are two consecutive working days for an employee who does not work weekends.

Documentation should confirm incapacity and relevant dates. Medical documents must be sent through the designated People Operations channel, not posted in a general chat or team channel.

## 4. Pay and entitlement

Paid sick-leave allowance is determined by the employee's work location, eligibility record, absence history, and applicable location addendum. An annual-leave balance must never be used as a substitute for missing sick-leave data.

The HR assistant may report a sick-leave value only when it comes from the dedicated synthetic entitlement record. It must state the record date and must not infer an entitlement from tenure alone.

## 5. Extended and repeated absence

An absence lasting five or more consecutive scheduled working days is an extended absence and must be referred to People Operations for case management. This referral is supportive and does not imply wrongdoing.

Where an employee has repeated short absences, a manager may arrange an attendance conversation. No automated system may characterize the pattern as abuse, recommend discipline, or infer a medical condition.

## 6. Privacy and access

Northstar collects only information necessary to administer leave and meet its obligations. Managers normally receive functional information such as dates and expected duration, not diagnosis details.

The HR assistant must not request, reproduce, or summarize medical records or diagnoses. If a user volunteers sensitive medical information, the assistant should advise them not to provide further details in chat and direct them to the secure People Operations channel.

## 7. Return to work and accommodations

After an extended absence, People Operations may coordinate a return-to-work discussion. Requests for workplace adjustments, accessibility support, or medical accommodations follow a confidential process and must be referred to People Operations.

The assistant may explain how to begin the process. It may not decide whether an adjustment is medically necessary or reasonable.

## 8. Emergency situations

For an immediate threat to health or safety, contact local emergency services first. The HR assistant is not an emergency or clinical service and should not delay urgent assistance by continuing policy analysis.

## 9. Escalation

Refer the matter to People Operations when:

- The absence reaches five consecutive scheduled working days.
- Eligibility or paid entitlement is unclear or disputed.
- An accommodation or phased return is requested.
- Sensitive medical information has been shared in an inappropriate channel.
- A manager is considering action based on an absence pattern.
- Policies or records conflict.
