# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Purpose and principles

Northstar reimburses reasonable and necessary costs incurred for an authorized business purpose. An item must satisfy both the category rules and the business-purpose test. A cost does not become reimbursable merely because it is below a monetary limit.

All amounts in this policy are synthetic demo values. Location or destination tables may impose a lower limit.

## 2. Submission and evidence

Claims should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date. A receipt is normally required for an item of HKD 200 or more unless a category below requires one at any amount.

Evidence must show the supplier, transaction date, amount, currency, and purchased items where relevant. Card slips without item detail may be insufficient for meals or entertainment.

Finance may accept a missing-receipt declaration in an exceptional case. Neither a manager nor the HR assistant can approve that exception.

## 3. Approval and separation of duties

The claimant's manager normally approves an in-policy expense. An amount over a stated limit also requires department-head approval unless valid pre-approval is already recorded.

No employee may approve their own expense. A manager's personal expense routes to their next-level manager. Splitting one purchase or event into several claims to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

Transactions that appear duplicated must be flagged for human review. A duplicate signal is not a finding of fraud and must not be presented as one.

## 4. Transport

Reasonable local public-transport costs for business travel are reimbursable with a receipt where the operator provides one. Ordinary commuting between home and the assigned office is not business travel.

A taxi or ride-hailing trip is limited to HKD 500. The claim requires an itemized receipt and business purpose. Department-head approval is required above HKD 500.

A taxi home after 22:00 is permitted when the late journey was caused by work, such as an approved client dinner or required late work. The late-night exception establishes a business reason; it does not remove the HKD 500 threshold or receipt requirement.

## 5. Meals

The daily meal limit for domestic business travel is HKD 350. The daily meal limit for international business travel is HKD 600, unless the destination rate table specifies a lower cap.

Itemized receipts are required. Personal meals during an ordinary office day are not reimbursable. Alcohol is excluded unless it forms part of approved client entertainment and is reasonable in context.

Meal limits are daily totals, not per-meal allowances. Several receipts on the same day must be aggregated before testing the threshold.

## 6. Hotels

The Hong Kong hotel limit is HKD 1,800 per night. International hotel limits come from the effective-dated destination rate table. The employee must provide the hotel invoice.

A designated conference hotel may exceed the cap when the cost was pre-approved. Taxes and mandatory hotel fees are assessed according to the destination table and should not be silently excluded from a comparison.

## 7. Client entertainment

Client entertainment is limited to HKD 1,500 per event. It requires attendee names, a business purpose, and an itemized receipt. Department-head approval is required above the limit.

The full event cost is tested against the limit. Dividing the total by the number of attendees or submitting separate parts does not avoid the threshold.

## 8. Home-office equipment

An employee may claim up to HKD 1,500 per policy year for items on the approved home-office equipment list. Manager approval and a receipt are required. The limit applies to the cumulative approved amount during the year.

Furniture, renovations, rent, utilities, and general internet costs are not covered unless People Operations has approved an accommodation or another policy expressly applies.

## 9. Mobile and roaming costs

Reasonable mobile or data-roaming costs incurred for business may be reimbursed with a provider statement. Reviewers should avoid collecting or displaying personal call details that are unnecessary to validate the cost.

## 10. Currency conversion

Use the recorded exchange rate on the transaction date. If that rate is unavailable, use Finance's monthly reference rate for the transaction month and disclose that assumption.

The original currency and amount must remain in the claim record. Rounding occurs only after conversion into the reimbursement currency.

## 11. Non-reimbursable and exceptional items

Personal costs, fines, penalties, unauthorized upgrades, and costs without a business purpose are not reimbursable. An employee should not describe employee-requested international remote-work travel as business travel.

Late submissions, missing evidence, above-limit amounts, and categories not defined here require human review. The assistant may identify the issue and approval route but cannot approve, reject, or accuse the claimant of misconduct.
